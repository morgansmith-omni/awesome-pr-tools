## Journalist Databases
*Find the right reporter on the right beat.*

* **[JournalistDB](https://journalistdb.com)** - 142,458+ journalists searchable by beat, publication, and location with verified contacts.
* [Muck Rack](https://muckrack.com) - Journalist database, monitoring, and PR analytics.
* [Cision](https://www.cision.com) - Enterprise PR platform with media database.
* [Prowly](https://prowly.com) - PR software with media database and pitch builder.
* [Roxhill Media](https://roxhillmedia.com) - UK-focused journalist database.
* [Notified](https://www.notified.com) - Press release distribution and media database.

## HARO & Source Platforms
*Reporters request quotes; respond to get coverage.*

* [Connectively](https://www.connectively.us) - Source requests from reporters (formerly HARO).
* [Qwoted](https://www.qwoted.com) - Source platform connecting experts with journalists.
* [HelpAB2BWriter](https://helpab2bwriter.com) - B2B-focused source requests for SaaS founders.
* [Featured](https://featured.com) - Q&A platform for getting quoted in articles.
* [SourceBottle](https://www.sourcebottle.com) - Australian and global source requests.

## Media Monitoring
*Track mentions of your brand, competitors, and topics.*

* [Meltwater](https://www.meltwater.com/en/products/media-monitoring) - Enterprise media monitoring and analytics.
* [Brand24](https://brand24.com) - SMB-friendly social and web mention monitoring.
* [Mention](https://mention.com) - Real-time brand monitoring across web and social.
* [Critical Mention](https://www.criticalmention.com) - Broadcast and online media monitoring.
* [Google Alerts](https://www.google.com/alerts) - Free mention monitoring (limited but useful).

## Press Release Distribution
*Wire services for announcements that need broad reach.*

* [PRWeb](https://www.prweb.com) - Mid-market press release distribution.
* [Newswire](https://www.newswire.com) - Distribution with media outreach.
* [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com) - Wire service used by public companies.
* [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com) - Established wire service.

## Pitch Tracking & Outreach
*Manage relationships and follow-ups with reporters.*

* [Pitchbox](https://pitchbox.com) - Outreach automation for PR and link building.
* [BuzzStream](https://www.buzzstream.com) - Outreach management with relationship CRM.
* [Postaga](https://postaga.com) - Outreach tool with built-in opportunity finder.

## Executive & Expert Contacts
*Sometimes the pitch goes to an operator, not a reporter — partnership announcements, award nominations, and agency new-business outreach.*

* [MessageCEO](https://messageceo.com) - 200,000+ verified CEO, CTO, and CMO emails searchable by company, industry, and location — for exec-level outreach and agency business development.
* [The Org](https://theorg.com) - Public org charts for thousands of companies; find the right executive before you pitch.

## Interview Recording & Transcription
*Turning the interview into quotable text.*

* [Descript](https://www.descript.com) - Audio and video editor where editing the transcript edits the recording.
* [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai) - Live transcription and shared notes, useful for briefings and panels.
* [Rev](https://www.rev.com) - Automated and human transcription with accuracy guarantees for publication-grade quotes.
* [TranscribeAnything](https://transcribeanything.app) - Browser-based transcription in 100+ languages with speaker detection and word-level timestamps; exports TXT, SRT, VTT, and JSON, and accepts YouTube URLs. No account required.
* [Trint](https://trint.com) - Transcription platform built around a collaborative editor, widely used in newsrooms.

## Newsletter & Audience Discovery
*Reach niche audiences through their writers, not their inboxes.*

* [SparkToro](https://sparktoro.com) - Discover what your audience reads, watches, and listens to.
* [Substack](https://substack.com) - Newsletter platform; excellent for finding niche writers.
* [Beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com) - Newsletter platform with discovery network.

## Podcast & Influencer Outreach
*Long-form audio and creator-led PR.*

* [Listen Notes](https://www.listennotes.com) - Podcast search engine with contact data.
* [Podchaser](https://www.podchaser.com) - Podcast database with credits and contacts.
* [Modash](https://www.modash.io) - Influencer search across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.
